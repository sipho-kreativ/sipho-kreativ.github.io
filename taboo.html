User Interface (UI) Design, Client Experience

Taboo Beauty Spa

The project involved a comprehensive UI/UX redesign of the Taboo Beauty Spa website, focused on creating a modern, intuitive and visually engaging user interface that reflects the salon’s brand identity and range of beauty services. The redesign prioritised user experience, responsive web design, accessibility and intuitive navigation, ensuring a seamless experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices. The updated interface was designed to make it easier for clients to browse beauty treatments, access service information and book appointments online. By improving the website’s visual hierarchy, navigation structure and interactive elements, the project aimed to create a more engaging digital customer experience, increase online interaction and encourage appointment bookings. The overall design combines modern web design principles, usability and visual consistency to strengthen Taboo Beauty Spa’s online presence and provide a more accessible and user-friendly platform for its clients.
