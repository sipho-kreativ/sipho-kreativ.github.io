Poster, Digital Illustration

Facebook

The project involved creating a digital illustration and film poster design inspired by The Social Network, with the objective of visually summarising the film’s narrative and exploring the events surrounding the early development of Facebook. The concept uses graphic design, digital illustration and visual storytelling to represent the key people, ideas and technological innovation that contributed to the creation of the social media platform. The artwork was developed as a contemporary movie poster, combining composition, typography and illustrative elements to communicate themes of technology, entrepreneurship, ambition, social media and digital innovation. The design aims to transform the complex story behind Facebook’s origins into an engaging visual narrative while demonstrating creative film poster design and digital graphic design techniques.
