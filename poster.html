Promotional Poster, Digital Illustration

The Ultimate Roc Band

Coldplay is widely recognised as one of the most influential bands in contemporary rock music, with a distinctive sound and visual identity that have shaped modern music culture. This project explores Coldplay through a creative digital illustration and promotional poster design, developed as a visual tribute to the band's musical achievements and lasting impact on the rock genre. The artwork uses graphic design, digital illustration and promotional poster design to capture the energy, creativity and iconic presence associated with Coldplay. The concept aims to celebrate the band's evolution and success while presenting a bold and engaging piece of music artwork that reflects their status as an influential contemporary rock band.
